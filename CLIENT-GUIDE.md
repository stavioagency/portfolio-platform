# Your Portfolio — Editing Guide

Welcome! This short guide shows you how to update your portfolio website yourself.
No technical knowledge needed — if you can use Instagram, you can use this. 🙂

---

## 1. Logging in

1. Go to your admin link (it ends with **`/admin`**).
2. Enter your **username** and **password**.
3. Click **Sign In**.

That's it — you're now in your dashboard.

> Tip: bookmark the `/admin` page so it's easy to find next time.

---

## 2. What each tab does

Your dashboard has tabs on the side. Here's what each one is for:

| Tab | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Profile** | Your personal info — name, photo, short bio, and extra fields. |
| **Home Page** | What shows on your public page — logo, banners, stats, and buttons. |
| **Projects** | Your work — add each project with a cover image, description, and gallery. |
| **Links** | Your social and contact links (Instagram, WhatsApp, email, etc.). |
| **Appearance** | Colors, theme, and fonts for your public page. |
| **Analytics** | See how many people visited your page and what they clicked. |
| **Account** | Your username, default language, and password. |

---

## 3. What to edit first

When you first log in, look for the **“Start here”** box on the Profile tab. It walks you through the basics:

1. Add your **name**
2. Add your **photo**
3. Add **one project**
4. **Preview** your site

Do those four things and your page already looks great. You can always come back and add more later.

---

## 4. Arabic and English content

Your page works in **both Arabic and English**, and visitors can switch between them.

- Use the **language button** (EN / ع) at the top to switch which language you're editing.
- **Arabic and English are saved separately.** When you switch language, the fields show that language's version — your other language is safe, not deleted.
- So: type your Arabic text, switch to English, type your English text. Both are kept.

---

## 5. Saving your changes

- Each tab has its own **Save** button near the bottom.
- After you edit something, click **Save**.
- You'll see **“Saved ✓”** when it works — and it stays there until your next edit.

On a phone, when you have unsaved changes the Save button stays within easy reach at the bottom of the screen.

---

## 6. What “unsaved changes” means

If you edit something but haven't saved yet, you'll see a small **orange dot** and **“You have unsaved changes.”**

- If you try to switch to another tab before saving, you'll get a quick reminder asking if you want to leave without saving.
- To keep your work: just click **Save** first. ✅

---

## 7. Previewing your live site

At the top of the menu there's a **“View live site”** button. Click it to open your public page in a new tab and see exactly what your visitors see.

Do this often — edit, save, then preview to check the result before sharing your link.

---

## 8. Uploading images

Anywhere you see an image box:

1. Click it and choose a picture from your device.
2. If asked, adjust the crop, then confirm.
3. Click **Save** on that tab.

Under each image box there's a short note telling you **where that image appears** and the **recommended size**. Following the recommended size keeps your images looking sharp.

---

## 9. What each image is for

| Image | Where it appears |
|---|---|
| **Profile photo** | Next to your name at the top of your page. |
| **Logo** | In the top corner of your page (instead of your first initial). |
| **Favicon** | The tiny icon in the browser tab. |
| **Banners** | The large sliding images near the top of your page. |
| **Project cover** | The thumbnail for a project in your projects grid. |
| **Project gallery** | Extra images shown inside a project when someone opens it. |
| **Share image (SEO)** | The preview picture shown when your link is shared on WhatsApp, Twitter, etc. |

---

## 10. How SEO fields work

SEO is what makes your page look good in Google and when your link is shared.

- The SEO fields are **completely optional.**
- If you **leave them empty**, we automatically use your name and bio — so you don't have to worry about them.
- If you want more control, you can write a custom page title, description, and share image. But it's totally fine to skip this.

---

## 11. Recovering your password

Forgot your password? No problem:

1. On the sign-in screen, click **“Forgot password?”**
2. Enter your **username or email**.
3. Check your email for a reset link.
4. Click the link, choose a new password, and you're back in.

> For security, we'll always say “we sent a link” even if the details don't match — so no one can guess your account.

---

## 12. Troubleshooting

**“Upload failed”**
- Check the file is an image (JPG or PNG) and under 5 MB.
- Try a slightly smaller image, or check your internet connection, then upload again.

**“Save failed”**
- Usually a brief internet hiccup. Wait a moment and click **Save** again.
- If it keeps happening, refresh the page and try once more.

**Forgot password**
- Use **“Forgot password?”** on the sign-in screen (see section 11).

**My changes aren't showing on the live site yet**
- Make sure you clicked **Save** on that tab.
- Refresh your public page — sometimes your browser shows an older version for a minute. A hard refresh (or opening it in a private window) usually fixes it.

---

## 13. What you don't need to worry about

- You **can't break anything permanently.** If something looks off, you can always change it back.
- **Empty rows won't show** on your public site — you don't have to fill in everything.
- You don't need to touch **Appearance** or **SEO** unless you want to — the defaults already look good.
- There's **nothing technical you need to manage** — no code, no settings behind the scenes. Just edit, save, and preview.
